Each castle sets them with [Domestic] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_Bugyou~Magistrates{1C}]].\n\nThere are 4 kinds: "Farming" raises yield, "Commerce" commerce, "Repair" defence, and "Recruitment" the number of officers.\n\nUp to 4 of each, 16 in all.\n\nOnce set, the same officers stay magistrates the next season.
@3466
A pact made by clans and minor powers. Clans with a non-aggression pact cannot attack each other, and their armies may pass through each other's castles.\n\nA daimyo can propose one to another clan with [Diplomacy] - [Daimyo] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_GA_DA_Fusenkyoutei~Non-Aggression{1C}]]. Anyone, whatever their rank, can propose one to Gentry, Ikko, Monks and Pirates with [Diplomacy] - [Minor Powers] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_GA_SH_HO_Fusenkyoutei~Non-Aggression{1C}]].\n\nIt lasts 2 years, or half a year when made through a ceasefire order from the court or shogunate. Using [Non-Aggression] again before it ends extends it.
@3467

@3468
A {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Sakusen~tactic{1C}] for siege battles. It sets fire between gates, stopping enemy units from moving for a while.\n\nThe higher the officer's Intellect, the stronger the effect.
@3469
The number of soldiers in the unit an officer leads.\n\nThe maximum is set by the officer's fief: 10 times the fief (a fief of 100 means 1000 troops).\n\nBelow the maximum, troops recover every season. To recover them at once, use [Military] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_GU_Bohei~Recruit{1C}]] to add troops for gold and rice.
@3470
There are 5 troop types: Infantry, Pikes, Cavalry, Gunners and Mounted Gunners.\n\nIn field battles, each type is strong against the next along the arrows:\nInfantry/Gunners -> Pikes -> Cavalry/Mounted Gunners -> Infantry/Gunners\n\nA unit fighting a type it is strong against gains attack and cuts down enemy troops more easily.
@3471
One of the skills an officer may have.\n\nAn officer with this skill excels at negotiation, and does better than others when asking other clans or minor powers for something through [Diplomacy].
@3472

@3473
A castle surrounded by an army. An army that reaches an enemy castle or base besieges it automatically. A besieged castle loses its seasonal income, its morale stops recovering, and its defence slowly drops.\n\nThe castle's clan and the besieging army's clan are then "{1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Tekitai~at war{1C}]".\n\nIn the Military phase, an army besieging an enemy castle starts a siege battle with [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_GU_GUNZEI_Chikarazeme~Assault{1C}]].
@3474
A {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Sakusen~tactic{1C}] for siege battles. It sets fire near a gate, stopping the enemy's [Ram] and [Defend] for a while.\n\nThe higher the officer's Intellect, the stronger the effect.
@3475
A minor power's strength in holding its base.\n\nWhen a clan army uses [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_GU_GUNZEI_Chikarazeme~Assault{1C}]] on a minor power's base, the higher its defence strength, the harder the base is to take.\n\nThe larger the minor power, the higher its defence strength.
@3476
A successful trade voyage brings gold or treasures. Trade with [Visit] - [Minor Powers] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_GA_SH_HO_Bouekiirai~Trade{1C}]].\n\nThe ship returns to Japan from abroad after half a year and hands the treasures and gold to the castle lord who asked. If it is wrecked, nothing is gained.\n\nA voyage costs 1000 gold, or 500 with a friendly pirate band. With an envoy of "Pirate" origin it costs even less.
@3477
A castle has 3 baileys (main, second and third), each with its own defence.\n\nThe higher the defence, the harder the castle is to take.\n\nDefence can be raised by appointing a repair magistrate with [Domestic] - [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_Bugyou~Magistrates{1C}]]. Appointing an officer with high Politics or the "Repair" skill raises it efficiently.
@3478
An event that occurs in autumn.\n\nCastles in a province with a bumper harvest get more rice income that year.
@3479
Roads linking your own castles (bases), and roads from your castles to adjacent enemy castles, are called supply lines.\n\nAn army that leaves its supply lines gets no rice, and its officers' morale falls. Supply lines also affect strategy in many other ways.\n\nA minor power can be chosen for diplomacy or [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_Baibai~Power Trade{1C}]] only if its base is within your supply lines or in the same province.\n\n[{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_JO_Busshiyusou~Transport{1C}]] and [{1C}[{1C}I~REF_SE_NA_JO_Tenpou~Transfer{1C}]] work only between castles linked by supply lines.
@3480
A {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Sakusen~tactic{1C}] for siege battles. It hurls fire pots at the enemy unit being fought, reducing its troops.\n\nThe higher the officer's Intellect, the stronger the effect.
@3481
Clans in a pact ({1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Fusenkyoutei~non-aggression pact{1C}], {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Gunjidoumei~military alliance{1C}], {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Houimou~encirclement{1C}]) with you, and clans and minor powers in a {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Koninkankei~marriage alliance{1C}] or {1C}[{1C}I~REF_V_Juuzokukankei~vassal relationship{1C}] with you, are your "allies". @3482

@3483
A clan's reputation. Refusing a ceasefire order from the court or shogunate lowers it.
@3484

@3485
Every officer has ambition. The more ambitious an officer, the likelier he is to defect; the more ambitious a castle lord, the likelier gekokujo. Ambition cannot be seen as a number.\n\nIt rises when his fief is cut, his treasures are taken or he is tempted by stratagems. Raising his fief or giving him treasures lowers it.
@3486

If you are the army's commander-in-chief you command the whole army; if not, you command only the units from your castle.\n\nA defeated army disbands and its officers return to their castles.

Each castle lord has a friendship with every other castle lord and minor power.\n\nThe higher it is, the better diplomacy and the like go with them.\n\n[Diplomacy] depends heavily on the friendship between clans, which is the friendship between their daimyo.

@3494

@3497

Give another castle a good impression of your clan.\n\nOn success, friendship between your daimyo and the target castle rises.\n\nAn envoy with high Politics and the Eloquence skill raises it more easily.\n\nIt succeeds more easily the higher the friendship between your castle and the target, and the lower that between the target and its daimyo. @3498
Ask another castle for gold.\n\nOn success, you receive a tenth of the gold the castle holds.\n\nThe higher the friendship between your castle and the target, the likelier success. An envoy with high Politics and the Eloquence or Plea skill helps further.
@3499
Slander a daimyo's overlord, or a castle lord's daimyo, to them.\n\nOn success, friendship falls between the target clan and its overlord clan, or between the target castle and its daimyo.\n\nIt succeeds more easily the higher your friendship with the target, and the lower the target's friendship with the one slandered. An envoy with high Intellect helps further.

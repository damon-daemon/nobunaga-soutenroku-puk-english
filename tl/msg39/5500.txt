Welcome to the world of Nobunaga no Yabou: Soutenroku! Here is a brief introduction to how to play. Let us start with an overview of the game.
@5714
You become one of the daimyo or castle lords of the Warring States era and aim to unify the realm. Press the {1B}PA button here for a more detailed explanation.
@5715
You become one of the daimyo or castle lords of the Warring States era and aim to unify the realm.
@5716
You clear the game when you, or the daimyo clan you belong to, unifies the land. If you die with no retainers at all, the game is over.
@5717
You can play as a daimyo, corps commander or castle lord. A daimyo rules the whole clan, a corps commander several castles, a castle lord only their own castle. Lords obey their commander, commanders their daimyo.
@5718
As a castle lord under a daimyo, you can serve loyally and rise in rank, or bide your time and go independent as a daimyo. But independence needs an eye for the right moment.
@5719
Castle lords and corps commanders don't just obey orders; they can also make proposals to their commander or daimyo. Commanders propose to the daimyo at "council", where lobbying beforehand counts.
@5720
The game alternates between two phases: the "Strategy phase" and the "Military phase". In the Strategy phase you handle politics and diplomacy; in the Military phase, battles and more.
@5721
Phases turn over each season (3 months). In the Strategy phase you give orders at the start of the season. The Military phase runs in real time, and you can give orders every half month.
@5722
If you scheme in the Strategy phase and shake the loyalty of enemy officers, you may bring down a mighty foe from within. You can even outwit your allies to get ahead...
@5723
For anyone living in the Warring States era, battle is unavoidable. To unify the realm you must win many field battles and sieges in the Military phase. Send officers with high Leadership.
@5724
When certain conditions are met during the game, events occur: some tied to history, some to the seasons, and more. How many will you see?
@5725
In the Strategy phase you enrich your province, send ninja on plots and schemes, poach officers and conduct diplomacy. With your tongue alone you can even take an enemy castle.
@5726
Enrich the province, secure supplies and always be ready for battle: these are a castle lord's basics. Domestic affairs are run by appointing magistrates; able officers raise your strength efficiently.
@5727
Troops are replenished each season, but if you need troops quickly, use [Recruit]. It is also important to set the troop type each officer leads with [Organise] in advance.
@5728
Spying and plots with ninja are the very secret of victory. A capable ninja can sometimes do more than an officer. Stay on good terms with the ninja bands at all times.
@5729
With [Visit] and [Diplomacy] you can negotiate with other daimyo and castle lords: pay tribute to a great power to survive, threaten a weak one, or even urge someone to rebel...
@5730
In the Military phase you send armies out of your castles to fight field battles and sieges. If a defection or independence is ready, you can carry it out here.
@5731
Each castle can send out only one army. When and where to send it...? Always keep an eye on the neighbouring provinces so as not to miss a chance of victory.
@5732
When armies of different clans meet on the map, a field battle begins. A field battle is a contest of strength between units: push the enemy units back into their headquarters to win. Troop matchups are the key.
@5733
When an army besieging an enemy castle launches an [Assault], a siege battle begins. It lasts until the attackers take the main bailey or one side retreats. Unit morale greatly decides the outcome.
@5734
The map also has bases of minor powers; besiege and take one and you can make it subordinate. Each kind of minor power has its own traits. Win them over to widen your strategy.
@5735
Press the {1B}PK button to open the Options menu, where you can save, load and change settings. There are also explanations of each command and a feature for creating your own officers.
@5736
To stop playing, choose [Save Play Data] from the Options menu. If you quit without saving, you cannot resume. You cannot save during battle.
@5737
If there is a command or term you don't understand, try pressing the {1B}PE button for a more detailed explanation of commands and terms.
@5738
With the Power-Up Kit you can create and register treasures and events as well as officers. Make new treasures and events and have them appear in the game.
@5739
Your retainers' abilities...? The situation nearby...? To call up information, press the {1B}PX button for the nearby situation, or choose [Info] for all kinds of lists.
@5740
The "key guide" at the bottom right of the screen shows the controls. If you are unsure how to do something, do have a look at it.
@5741
That concludes our brief introduction to the world of Nobunaga no Yabou: Soutenroku. Use all your skill in war and strategy and write your own history of the Warring States. Good luck in battle!
